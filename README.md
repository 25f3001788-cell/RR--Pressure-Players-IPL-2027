# Rajasthan Royals - Who Performs Under Pressure? | IPL 2027 Auction Analysis

This is my first competition entry for the Unstop x Rajasthan Royals Challenge.

## The Question
Who delivers under pressure?

For the IPL 2027 Auction, I analysed which players actually perform when the pressure is at its peak, not just who scores the most runs.

## What I Did
- Analysed 270K+ balls in Power BI
- Created a Pressure Score for Batters and Bowlers
- Analysed Death Overs, Chase Pressure and High-Pressure Moments

## Key Findings
- 67K runs were scored in pressure overs alone - 24.83% of total runs
- Pressure Strike Rate: 150.59
- Top Pressure Batters: Tim David, Rinku Singh, Hardik Pandya, Tilak Varma, Rovman Powell, David Miller

## Conclusion
This is not just about runs, this is about temperament. In an auction, money should not go on runs, it should go on temperament.
